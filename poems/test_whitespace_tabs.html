Makerspace

 

Valentino's mad at Jonny.

Jonny sliced his finger

Now Valentino's got to

update the accident book.

 

Sean has his head down

in the corner

The soldering iron

cools

 

Pete and Jenny hunch over a laptop

Looks like a proposal

 

Andre snorts loudly. Rob rubs oil into his plane sole.

They are arguing about construction of identity again

Or sausages.

 

The kettle finishes boiling.

I distribute teas and go to watch my sculpture.

The shape hasn't quite fallen out of its shape yet.

 

Patrick Barry
